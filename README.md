## How some of the calculations work

**Pakistan salary tax (FY 2026-27, Tax Year 2027), an estimate for salaried individuals**

| Annual income (PKR) | Tax |
|---|---|
| Up to 600,000 | 0% |
| 600,001 - 1,200,000 | 1% of the amount above 600,000 |
| 1,200,001 - 2,200,000 | 6,000 + 11% above 1.2M |
| 2,200,001 - 3,200,000 | 116,000 + 20% above 2.2M |
| 3,200,001 - 4,100,000 | 316,000 + 25% above 3.2M |
| 4,100,001 - 5,600,000 | 541,000 + 29% above 4.1M |
| 5,600,001 - 7,000,000 | 976,000 + 32% above 5.6M |
| Above 7,000,000 | 1,424,000 + 35% above 7M |

Example: PKR 250,000 a month is PKR 3,000,000 a year, so the tax is 116,000 + 20% of 800,000 = PKR 276,000 a year (PKR 23,000 a month). Allowances, exemptions and employer deductions are not included. Confirm with your payroll department or the FBR.

**Land and gold units**
- 1 marla = 272.25 sq ft; 1 kanal = 20 marla = 5,445 sq ft (the marla can differ by region, so check your papers)
- 1 tola = 11.664 g; 1 tola = 12 masha = 96 ratti; 1 troy ounce = 31.1035 g

**Loan EMI (reducing balance):** EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of months.
